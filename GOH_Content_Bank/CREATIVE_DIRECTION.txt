GOH CREATIVE DIRECTION AND TWO WEEK REVIEW

Open with a photograph or real footage. Carousel first slides and Reel covers carry a small Paid for by National Hemp Association credit. Keep faces, labels, machinery and materials visible. Preserve natural color. Do not add a dark wash across the whole image.

Let Morgan’s This is hemp material unfold through the inner slides and captions. Educational frames use Montserrat, clear spacing and selective copy placement. Use cream text areas or soft fades only where the words need contrast. Consistency comes from the typography, photographic choices and voice rather than repeating every design element on every frame.

The campaign closing is always Plan. Don’t Ban. followed by Tell Congress: Support the Goodness of Hemp. Follow Goodness of Hemp remains supporting copy rather than replacing that closing. The website journey remains TheGoodnessOfHemp.org > Tell Congress > review the message > send. Keep the GOH logo and lime button treatment on closing cards and direct Friday advocacy posts. The NHA attribution remains on every graphic and video. Add actual native link, mention or question stickers to Stories; a drawn label is not an interactive sticker.

Keep the Goodness of Hemp name and handle during this cycle. GOH’s Account Status has not been inspected for this package. Before the first post, review Account Status and recommendation eligibility in Instagram. Record any affected content or profile element and the reason shown. Use the available review route if a decision appears mistaken. Low reach alone does not establish a restriction, and eligibility does not guarantee distribution.

Use a small number of subject-specific hashtags. The captions include relevant agriculture, fiber, food, manufacturing and transparency tags. Hemp remains explicit where it describes the subject. Do not add an unrelated cannabis or CBD tag bundle to every post, disguise regulated-product promotions, or treat another handle as an eligibility fix.

Keep five feed concepts per week and daily Stories as the starting cadence. Coordinate one partner email or newsletter slot weekly and seek an agreed original collaboration with a participating farmer, processor, manufacturer or coalition partner. No coauthor invitation, publication, email or paid spend has been executed by this package.

Use the first two weeks for an exploratory review. Record a recent comparable baseline and evaluate every post after the same seven-day window. Capture reach, non-follower reach where available, shares, saves, watch time for Reels, profile visits and follows. The blank scorecard is a reporting aid, not a native scheduling import. Do not enter zeros when a metric is unavailable. Compare shares or saves relative to reach; do not compare a new post’s 48-hour result with an older post’s lifetime total.

Track direct social referrals, partner email clicks and verified completed Tell Congress actions separately. If source attribution does not persist into CongressPlus, report referrals and total completed actions separately. Several creative and distribution changes are being made together, so this small test cannot isolate hashtags or prove algorithmic suppression. At the end of week two, use the findings to choose the strongest subjects and openings for the next cycle.

General farm photographs illustrate the wider agriculture and crop-rotation story. Do not identify unconfirmed people or crops as hemp production. Product photos do not establish hemp composition, regulatory approval, dose, current availability or medical benefits. Source and context notes remain in the content bank.
