# The Goodness of Hemp® coalition social kit

## Share once a week. Join the final three days.

| Suggested date | Use this folder | What to share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| November 10 | `01-nov-10-launch/` | Launch feed graphic and matching Story. |
| November 17 | `02-nov-17-farmers/` | Feed graphic alone, or use it as the first slide of the four-slide carousel. Add the Story. |
| November 24 | `03-nov-24-coalition/` | Feed graphic, three-slide carousel, or vertical Reel. Beverage and gummy partners can use the matching alternate feed graphics and Stories. Add your own approved business story if you have one. |

| December 10 | `04-dec-10-final/` | Final feed graphic and matching Story. Share this alongside the last email. |

## Post the files

- Feed and carousel files are 1080 × 1350. Upload the `feed.png` first, then each numbered slide in order.
- Stories, the Reel and its cover are 1080 × 1920. For a Story, add the Tell Congress link in the open space below the button. For a feed post or Reel, give readers a clickable route to the same action page.
- Copy the post text from [captions.md](captions.md). [manifest.json](manifest.json) has alt text and the file order for both carousels.
- Use **[Tell Congress](https://takeaction.io/nationalhempassociation/tell-congress-support-the-goodness-of-hemp/)**. Readers can review their message before sending it to their lawmakers.
- December 10 is the final campaign push. December 11 is the federal deadline referenced in the campaign. Check the timing before publishing the countdown if congressional action changes it.

## Add your logo or tell your own story

Open [customize.html](customize.html) in a current browser. Select the graphic and upload your logo in PNG, JPG or WebP format. Use a mark that is legible on white. Download a full-size PNG and repeat for the versions you want to post. Keep the campaign mark and the supplied NHA attribution on the shared artwork.

For a [business quote card](customize.html#quote), add a photo you have permission to share, the speaker’s name and business, and a short quote the speaker approves. Use [the recording guide](03-nov-24-coalition/record-your-story.md) for a short owner or farmer Reel. The supplied Reel is a GOH campaign edit with on-screen text. It works with sound off.

## Put the kit on the coalition page

Keep the folder layout intact when you upload it. The paths in [manifest.json](manifest.json) are relative to this folder. The customizer and its `customize-assets/` folder belong together. The editor runs in a partner’s browser and uses local files for logo and photo uploads.

The campaign photography is from the GOH content bank. The beverage alternate and Reel use imagery from the GOH website’s campaign footage.
