# Record your business story

Film vertically at your farm, shop or facility. A person who works there can speak straight to camera. Give their name, business and location in the caption. Keep it to about 15–20 seconds and add captions so viewers can follow with sound off.

> I’m [name] from [business] in [city and state]. We [say what you grow, make or sell]. If the rules change on December 11, [say one specific thing that could change for your products, team, customers or farm]. Congress still has time to act. Please add your voice.

Use one detail you can verify and are comfortable making public. The speaker should approve the final wording and footage. In the caption, include the [Tell Congress link](https://takeaction.io/nationalhempassociation/tell-congress-support-the-goodness-of-hemp/).
