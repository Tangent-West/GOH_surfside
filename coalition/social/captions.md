# Coalition partner captions

**Tell Congress**  
https://takeaction.io/nationalhempassociation/tell-congress-support-the-goodness-of-hemp/

Copy the caption and the Tell Congress link where your platform makes links clickable. On Instagram, use your profile link for the feed and add the action link to the Story. Each caption is written for organizations to share in their own voice.

## November 10 · Launch

What will the next 30 years look like for American hemp? What Congress does in the next 30 days can help shape the answer.

There are farmers making plans for another season, small businesses working to keep their doors open, and people across the country who want clear rules for hemp products. Congress has time to act before December 11. Tell your representative to support The Goodness of Hemp and the proposed hemp certainty language in the next funding bill.

Tell Congress. Plan. Don’t Ban.

**Short version**  
The next 30 days can help shape the next 30 years of American hemp. Stand with farmers and small businesses. Tell Congress to act before December 11. Plan. Don’t Ban.

## November 17 · Farmers and policy

A farmer has to order seed, commit acreage and find a buyer long before a crop goes in the ground. A processor needs to know what’s coming in and who will buy it. Policy uncertainty reaches all the way back to the farm.

Ask your representative to support the proposed hemp certainty language in the next federal funding bill and a longer-term plan with clear rules and consumer safeguards.

Tell Congress before December 11. Plan. Don’t Ban.

**Short version**  
Seed. Acreage. Buyers. Hemp farmers have to make those decisions months ahead. Ask Congress for hemp certainty in the next funding bill. Speak up before December 11.

## November 24 · The businesses behind hemp

Behind a hemp product is a chain of people who grew it, processed it, tested it or found a way to put it to work. For some hemp businesses, changes to federal rules could mean losing products they rely on or being forced to close.

Congress can still act before December 11. Tell your representative what a workable plan would mean for the people and businesses in your community. Already acted? Pass the link to someone who can add their voice.

Tell Congress. Plan. Don’t Ban.

**Short version**  
Some hemp businesses could be forced to close. Congress can still act before December 11. Tell your representative what a workable plan would mean for your community.

**For your own business story**  
Start with one sentence about what your team does and one specific, approved detail about what the rules could change for you. Close with “Ask Congress to act before December 11” and the campaign link.

## November 24 · Beverage alternate

Behind every can are the people who blend, test, pack and ship it. If Congress doesn’t act before December 11, new federal rules could take many of these drinks off shelves. For the businesses that make them, that puts jobs and years of work at risk.

Ask your representative to include the proposed hemp certainty language in the next funding bill and work toward clear rules for adult hemp products, including testing, accurate labels and age safeguards.

Tell Congress now. Plan. Don’t Ban.

**Short version**  
Many hemp drinks could come off shelves under new federal rules. Think about the people who blend, test, pack and ship them. Ask Congress to act before December 11.

## November 24 · Low-dose gummy alternate

The new federal limit applies per container. Many low-dose hemp gummies could lose their federal hemp status if Congress doesn’t act before December 11. For the people who make, test and sell them, that means decisions about products, jobs and whether they can stay open.

Ask your representative to give responsible hemp businesses time to plan and support clear rules for adult products, including testing, accurate labels and age safeguards.

Congress can still act. Tell Congress now. Plan. Don’t Ban.

**Short version**  
Small dose. Big stakes. Many low-dose hemp gummies could lose federal hemp status. Congress can still act. Tell your representative to speak up for hemp before December 11.
