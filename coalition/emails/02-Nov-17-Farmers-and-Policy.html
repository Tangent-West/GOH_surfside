Long before a crop goes in the ground, farmers have to order seed, line up buyers and decide how much acreage they can commit. Processors and manufacturers make investments months ahead, too. They all need to know the rules they’ll be working under.

Congress has three possible routes. It can include hemp certainty language in a continuing resolution, address hemp in a broader spending package, or take up a stand-alone Goodness of Hemp Act. The immediate ask is to include the proposed language in the next CR.

Ask your representative to support The Goodness of Hemp and the proposed CR language before December 11. A long-term framework can give consumers clear labels and safeguards while giving farmers and responsible businesses a path to market.
