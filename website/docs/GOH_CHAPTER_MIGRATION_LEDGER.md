# GOH education chapter migration ledger

This ledger covers the seven native education pages in `website/data/chapter-content.js`. Each route uses the current GOH layout and Montserrat system, retains original-site photography, keeps film playback click-to-play, and replaces the former outbound/dead-end chapter links with an internal page.

## Status key

- **Migrated:** the source chapter block has a direct native section or card group.
- **Merged; source preserved:** closely related source blocks were combined for the new layout; their substantive subject matter and relevant original imagery remain present.
- **Qualified:** health, nutrition, performance, market, policy, or regulatory language was narrowed and paired with named sources rather than presented as an unqualified claim.

## Shared launch dependencies

- **Image rights and credits:** confirm public-use permission, attribution requirements, and approved credit lines for all 38 original-source chapter assets, including the Hemp Feed Coalition mark and any third-party photography.
- **Film rights and metadata:** confirm permission to embed every chapter YouTube film, final public titles/captions, thumbnails, and accessibility transcripts or captions.
- **Editorial approval:** obtain final stakeholder approval of the merged section structure, cross-chapter links, alt text, and qualified rewrites before production publication.
- **Legal and subject-matter review:** recheck cited rules, agency guidance, health language, feed approvals, building-code adoption, beverage-policy wording, warnings, and jurisdictional statements against launch-date sources. The chapter disclaimers do not replace that review.
- **Credits and source maintenance:** decide whether the production design should expose image credits in-page or through a shared credits page, and assign an owner for periodic link and regulatory review.
- **Live commerce:** production donation and merchandise checkout still require the approved recipient/account, checkout URL or provider integration, tax/receipt and fulfillment language, privacy/terms destinations, and an end-to-end live transaction test. No chapter page itself processes a payment.
